About Manifest Texas

We book and promote live shows across Austin, Dallas, San Antonio, and Houston: bass, techno, and hip-hop nights built with the artists, agencies, and venues behind them.

Manifest Texas is led by a promoter and talent buyer with 25 years of Texas shows behind him. We partner with emerging and established artists, labels, and agencies to grow their audience across the state.
